On October 17, 2023 at 9:24 AM PT a Waymo Autonomous Vehicle ("Waymo AV") operating in San Francisco, California was in a collision involving a cyclist on Market Street at Gough Street.

The Waymo AV was in autonomous mode on eastbound Market Street preparing to make a right turn onto southbound Gough Street and had stopped to yield, first for pedestrians to cross Gough Street and then for a cyclist approaching from behind traveling eastbound on the sidewalk of Market Street. As the pedestrians finished crossing Gough Street but before the cyclist had reached Gough Street, the Waymo AV test driver took manual control of the Waymo AV and began to accelerate and perform the right turn onto Gough Street. After the Waymo AV entered the crosswalk, the cyclist entered the crosswalk and made contact with the passenger's rear quarter panel of the Waymo AV. At the time of the impact, the Waymo AV's Level 4 ADS was not engaged and a test driver was operating the Waymo AV in manual mode. The Waymo AV sustained damage.
