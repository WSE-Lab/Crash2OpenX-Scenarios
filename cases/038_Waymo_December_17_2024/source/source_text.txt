On December 17, 2024 at 1:08 PM PT a Waymo Autonomous Vehicle (“Waymo AV”) operating in San Francisco, California was in a collision involving a heavy truck on Van Ness Avenue at O'Farrell Street. The Waymo AV was in autonomous mode traveling north in the rightmost of two lanes on Van Ness Avenue when it partially entered the left adjacent lane to make room for a cyclist on its right who was traveling in the same direction. A heavy truck behind in the left adjacent lane proceeded forward, and the Waymo AV test driver transitioned to manual mode immediately before the rear driver side of the Waymo AV made contact with the front passenger side of the heavy truck. At the time of the impact, the Waymo AV's Level 4 ADS was not engaged and a test driver was operating the Waymo AV in manual mode. Both vehicles sustained damage. [ ] Additional information attached.
