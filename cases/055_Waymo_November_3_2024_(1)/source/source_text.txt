On November 3, 2024 at 1:18 PM PT a Waymo Autonomous Vehicle (“Waymo AV”) operating in San Francisco, California was in a collision involving an ambulance on Cesar Chavez Street at Guerrero Street.

The Waymo AV was traveling east in the leftmost of two lanes on Cesar Chavez Street. While the traffic light was yellow, the Waymo AV began to cross the intersection with Guerrero Street. As the Waymo AV was crossing the intersection, an ambulance traveling north on Guerrero Street (without lights and sirens activated) began to make a right turn onto eastbound Cesar Chavez Street. While the ambulance completed its right turn it crossed into the lane the Waymo AV was traveling in and the front driver side of the ambulance then made contact with the rear passenger side door of the Waymo AV. At the time of the impact, the Waymo AV’s Level 4 ADS was engaged in autonomous mode. The Wamo AV sustained damage.
